Modifications by Octopool, September 2026:
- Removed the manufacturer emblems, badges, wheel center logos, license plates and the front plate holder;
  the front grille pattern was completed where the emblem used to be.
- Replaced all materials and textures with plain colors (grey paint, satin black trims, glass, alloy wheels).
- Separated the hood, driver-side doors, driver-side wheels and front fender into hinged parts.
- Added an engine with timing belt and pulleys, an air filter and a front shock absorber with coil spring.
- Added an engine bay (splash shield, inner fenders, strut towers, firewall, radiator, battery, reservoirs),
  door trim panels on the driver-side doors and a cabin floor.
- Wheels: the original rim (spokes, lug nuts, center cap without the logo) reduced to a 17-inch size, with a new
  rim barrel and a new tire modeled by Octopool; the same wheel is used on all four corners.
- Simplified and quantized the meshes for web delivery, keeping the original vertex normals (the painted body,
  glass and rear trims keep the original mesh).
- Added a scroll-driven exploded view and interactive part markers.
